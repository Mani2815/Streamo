How Streamo Works

1. Source APIs & Ingestion

Connect any REST API. Streamo workers automatically poll your endpoints and push raw records to the system.

2. Message Broker (Kafka)

Raw records land in Apache Kafka topics, providing durable, scalable, real-time message buffering.

3. Stream Processing (Spark)

Apache Spark Structured Streaming reads from Kafka, applies quality schemas, and processes the continuous flow.

4. Storage & Observability

Clean data goes to PostgreSQL/MinIO, while the Streamo Dashboard instantly visualizes the throughput and quality metrics.
